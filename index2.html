Strict full-length exam
180 questions selected automatically · 5 sections · 42 minutes per section · 210 minutes total. Question selection is controlled by the system.

General instructions

  • Each question has its own response type. Read the instruction above the question.
  • Subject-wise practice lets you choose a question count from that subject’s available questions. Topic-wise practice filters questions to the selected subject and topic. Mixed question practice disables subject selection and uses the whole bank. Strict full-length mode automatically selects 180 questions, locks each 42-minute section, advances automatically, and does not allow returning to earlier sections.
  • For the sample NEET PG-style MCQs, scoring is +4 for correct, −1 for incorrect and 0 for unanswered. Other response types use the answer rules configured in the question bank.
  • Practice time is calculated from 180 questions in 210 minutes and rounded up to a full minute. Strict full-test mode requires at least 180 published questions (36 per section).
  • Your result is calculated in this browser. No central result database is included.
  • After entering your details, the test begins directly. Question-bank tools are not shown on this student page.
